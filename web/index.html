How to play

Goal: have the fewest points in your hand.

A = 1 · 2–10 = face value · J = 11 · Q = 12 · Red K = −1 · Black K = 13 · Joker = 0. Points are shown at the bottom right of each card.

You start with four cards, two of them visible only to you. Memorize them — you can't see them again once the game starts.

On your turn, draw from the deck or the discard pile. Then either discard the card (to use its power) or replace one of your own cards with it. A card drawn from the discard pile can't use its power.

Powers

  • 7, 8 – peek at one of your own cards
  • 9, 10 – peek at someone else's card
  • J, Q – swap any two cards without looking
  • K – look at two cards, then decide whether to swap them

Flipping

Any time there's a card on the discard pile you can tap your own or someone else's card to flip it. Everyone sees the flipped card for a moment. If it matches the top discard, the flip is correct and the card goes to the discard pile: flipping your own card shrinks your hand (good!); flipping someone else's lets you give them one of your cards in its place. A wrong flip gives you a random extra card from the deck, kept face-down. More than 6 cards and you lose. Only one flip can succeed per discard, and you can't flip on your own discard.

Winning

When you think you have the fewest points, press Call Cambio on your turn before drawing. Everyone else gets one more turn, and your cards are protected. If you have strictly fewer points than everyone else you win; if anyone ties or beats you, they win.
